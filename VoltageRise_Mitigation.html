Mitigation 1 — Q absorption
Inverters switch to reactive power absorption mode (Q absorption). Pulls voltage down by reducing the reactive component of export current.
−1.4 kV effect
Mitigation 2 — OLTC tap changer
The on-load tap changer at the grid transformer adjusts its turns ratio automatically to bring bus voltage back within limits.
−1.6 kV effect
Why this matters
Statutory limit: ±6% of nominal (Electricity Hazards from Trees Regs)
Over-voltage damages consumer equipment and triggers relay disconnects
WEL requires a harmonic study and voltage management plan pre-connection
Both mitigations work together — Q absorption is fast, OLTC is continuous
